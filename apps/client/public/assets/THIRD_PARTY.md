# Third-party prototype assets

## Pixel Crawler — Anokolisa
Source: https://anokolisa.itch.io/free-pixel-art-asset-pack-topdown-tileset-rpg-16x16-sprites

Only files used by the JUNQVERSE prototype are vendored here.
The original terms are preserved at `assets/pixel-crawler/TERMS.txt`.
They allow use in commercial, study, and other functional projects, modification,
and optional attribution. The assets themselves may not be sold as a final asset product.

## Pimen spell packs
Reviewed as visual references:
- Holy Spell Effect
- Ice Spell Effect 01
- Wood Spell Effect

Their raw sprites are not vendored in this public repository because the author
allows use/modification in projects but disallows redistribution of the sprites.
The prototype uses procedural telegraphs plus CC0 VFX textures instead.
